About TestforUHS

A free study space for medical students to learn, review, and self-test.

Our Mission

TestforUHS was built to make studying a little easier. It gives medical students a simple place to review questions, practice like a real exam, and keep track of the topics they miss—without paying for access.

How TestforUHS Stays Free & Funded

We want this platform to stay open and free for everyone, so we cover the cost of hosting, the domain, and maintenance in a simple and transparent way:

1. Light Advertising (Google AdSense)

  • Where ads appear: Ads only appear at the bottom of the Home page and on the Session Results page.
  • No interruptions during study: We do not place ads inside active quiz or study screens, so you can stay focused.
  • Filtered content: We keep the ads limited and block adult content, gambling, clickbait, and anything misleading.

2. Optional Support from the Community (KHQR Donations)

If TestforUHS has been helpful for your studies, you can choose to support the platform through the Support Us button with KHQR. It is completely optional, and it does not give anyone extra benefits or special access.

Our Privacy Promise

Your study progress, missed questions, and test scores are stored locally inside your browser (localStorage). We do not track your personal study choices or sell student data to third parties.
