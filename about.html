About TestforUHS

A free place for medical students to review what they know, practise, and find the topics they want to revisit.

Our Mission

TestforUHS started with a simple idea: studying is easier when the questions you need are easy to reach. It gives medical students a place to review, practise like an exam, and keep track of the topics they want to revisit, without putting the study tools behind a paywall.

How TestforUHS Stays Free & Funded

We want the platform to stay open and free, so we help cover hosting, the domain, and maintenance in two straightforward ways:

1. Light Advertising (Google AdSense)

  • Where ads appear: Ads are enabled only on selected pages with substantial publisher content. They do not appear on the homepage selector, active study sessions, account pages, or feedback pages.
  • No interruptions during study: We keep ads out of active quiz and study screens so you can stay focused.
  • Careful placement: We keep advertising limited and do not intentionally promote adult content, gambling, clickbait, or misleading offers.

2. Optional Support from the Community (KHQR Donations)

If TestforUHS has helped with your studies, you can support the platform through the Support Us button with KHQR. It is completely optional and does not unlock special features or access.

Our Privacy Promise

Your study progress, missed questions, and test scores stay in your browser (IndexedDB). We do not sell your study data or use it to build a personal profile about you.

Have a question about the platform or its study material? Email us at testforuhs@gmail.com.
