Privacy Policy

At TestforUHS, visitor privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect and how it is used.

1. Advertising & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info.

2. Local Storage

TestforUHS uses your browser's localStorage exclusively to save quiz progress, missed question logs, language settings, and theme preferences directly on your device. This data is never transmitted to external servers.
