## What's New in v1.1.0

TestforUHS is now faster, more reliable, and easier to use across phones, tablets, and computers.

- **Faster studying:** Questions load efficiently, and Study Mode renders large sets in small batches.
- **Smarter data handling:** Saved progress, missed questions, analytics, and question tracking use safer, more consistent storage.
- **Offline study packs:** Download professor question sets for offline use. Manage saved packs from My Account.
- **Mobile and tablet support:** Improved touch targets, responsive layouts, navigation, and offline compatibility.
- **Theme support:** Privacy Policy and Terms pages now follow the saved Light/Dark mode.
- **Better compatibility:** Subject files with accented names continue to load correctly.

Quiz sessions still start fresh each time. Download offline packs while online, then open the site from HTTPS to use them without internet.
