## What's New in v1.0.2

We improved the study system behind the scenes to make repeated studying more reliable and efficient:

- **Faster repeated loading:** Question files are reused after they have already been loaded instead of being downloaded again unnecessarily.
- **Smoother study progress:** Saved study progress now uses one consistent storage system, making resume and restart behavior more reliable.
- **Safer saved data:** Existing missed questions, study progress, and analytics are checked when the new storage system is introduced. Invalid saved entries are ignored safely.
- **More consistent question tracking:** Question and professor records now use consistent identifiers, helping prevent duplicated saved questions and mismatched progress.
- **Improved subject compatibility:** Subjects with accented names, such as **Sémiologie** and **Santé Publique**, continue to load correctly.

These improvements do not change the website's appearance, but they make study sessions and repeat visits work more efficiently.

## What's New in v1.0.3

### Faster Study Mode
- Study mode now loads questions in small batches instead of placing the entire question set on the page at once.
- Long study sessions should open faster and use less memory.
- More questions are loaded automatically as you move down the page.
- The existing answer feedback, automatic scrolling, and latest-unanswered-question controls still work as before.

Quiz sessions are unchanged and still start fresh each time.

## What's New in v1.0.4

### Offline Study Packs
- You can now download a professor's question pack to this browser for offline study.
- Saved packs are kept in IndexedDB so they keep working without an internet connection.
- The app will prefer a cached pack when the network is slow or unavailable.
- You can remove individual packs or clear the whole offline list from My Account.
- Offline support is intentionally lightweight and keeps the current study flow intact.

This is the first step toward a fuller offline learning experience without changing the existing design or quiz flow.
