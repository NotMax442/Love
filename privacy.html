Privacy Policy

Welcome to TestforUHS! We built this platform so medical students can focus entirely on studying and practicing for exams without worrying about accounts, tracking, or privacy hassles. Here is a simple, transparent look at how the site works and how your data is handled.

1. No Sign-Ups or Accounts

You can jump straight into studying without ever creating an account. TestforUHS does not collect, store, or ask for any personal details like your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Your Progress Stays on Your Device

To let you resume quizzes where you left off and practice offline, TestforUHS saves your study activity locally in your browser (using localStorage and sessionStorage). This includes:

  • Study Progress & Resume Points: Remembers which question you were on so you can pick up right where you left off.
  • Missed Questions: Saves questions you answered incorrectly so you can revisit and master them later.
  • Preferences: Keeps track of your display and language settings.

All of this data stays 100% on your own phone or computer. It is never sent to us or any external database. If you clear your browser history or site data, your saved progress will simply reset.

3. Visual Assets & Fonts

To keep the site looking clean and loading fast, we use a couple of lightweight open-source web assets:

  • Google Fonts: For clean typography.
  • Lucide Icons: For interface icons.

These libraries load directly through standard web content delivery networks when your browser opens the site.
