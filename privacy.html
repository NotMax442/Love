Privacy Policy

Welcome to TestforUHS. We want studying to feel simple, so you can use the platform without creating an account or handing over personal information. This policy explains what stays on your device, what is loaded from outside services, and how your privacy is handled.

1. No Sign-Ups or Accounts

You can start studying right away without signing up. TestforUHS does not ask you for personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number, and we do not maintain user accounts on the site.

2. Your Progress Stays on Your Device

Some features work better when the site remembers your progress. To let you continue study sessions, review missed questions, and use supported offline features, TestforUHS stores this information locally in your browser. Most study data is kept in IndexedDB; small preferences and temporary session details may use localStorage or sessionStorage. This includes:

  • Study Progress & Resume Points: Saves your place in a study session so you can come back to it later.
  • Missed Questions: Keeps questions you answered incorrectly available for later review.
  • Preferences: Remembers settings such as your theme and language choice.

This study data stays on your phone or computer and is not sent to our servers or stored in an external account database. If you clear this site's browser data, your saved progress and preferences will be removed.

3. Visual Assets & Fonts

To make the site easier to read and use, we load a few lightweight web assets from established third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: Provides the typeface used across the site.
  • Lucide Icons: Provides many of the interface icons.

Your browser requests these assets directly from their content delivery networks when the site loads. Those providers may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, as part of serving the files. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.
